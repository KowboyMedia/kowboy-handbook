## 8 `[handbook]` When you correct an agent, should the first correction be remembered so the second one becomes a rule without you repeating yourself, and should the handbook be tidied once a month?

Asked 2026-10-30, rewritten in plain words 2026-10-31 (audit findings C7, D2). Today a first
correction is remembered only inside the thread it happened in, so the same correction is made
again and again before it becomes a rule, and the handbook only ever grows.

- a) **yes, both** (recommended): you correct any one thing at most twice, and the handbook stays
  short, with rules nobody breaks removed.
- b) **the monthly tidy only**: the handbook stays short, but you may still have to repeat a
  correction.
- c) **no**: as today.

Blocked: nothing today. The smaller option is b). Reply: a, b or c.

## 9 `[handbook]` May an agent send, change or move a client's data through connected services (mail, files, chat, shops, accounting) on its own?

Asked 2026-10-30, rewritten in plain words 2026-10-31 (audit finding E1). Sessions now carry many
connected services and nothing says what an agent may do through them.

- a) **no, read only** (recommended): an agent only reads through a connected service unless the
  task needs a write and says so beforehand, and a client's data never leaves the client's own
  systems. Nothing is sent or changed without you knowing.
- b) **yes**: agents use connected services as they see fit.

Blocked: nothing today. The smaller option is a). Reply: a or b.
