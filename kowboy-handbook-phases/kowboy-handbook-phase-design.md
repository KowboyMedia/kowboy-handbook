# Kowboy handbook: phase "Design"

Version 2026-11-17a.

## Design

When: something people look at is created or restyled. Never in a repository whose `AGENTS.md` says "Design: none".

- Size first. None (existing patterns): build, one picture on desktop and one on mobile, compare with a neighbouring page. Light (inside a pattern, adding something): build against `DESIGN.md` and check yourself once. Full (a new page or look): the loop.
- `DESIGN.md`, once per repository, written by an agent from references the operator names: colours, type, spacing, rules for components, tone, and what to avoid. Real content from the start.
- The loop: build one section at a time; a second agent looks at desktop and mobile and scores order, contrast, spacing, every state and keyboard use; at most three passes. For a new look, two or three finished candidates side by side, and the operator picks.

End of the phase.
